OUR MISSION

Strong engineering and manufacturing infrastructure.

60+
YEARS OF INNOVATION

Since 1964, HASSAN has combined its mass-production experience with advanced engineering infrastructure to design and build the technological production lines, automation systems, machines and molds it needs entirely in-house.

Thanks to the importance it places on R&D, HASSAN has sustained its development, growth and strength. It now considers it a duty to make the technological expertise it has built in-house available to domestic and national industrial organizations, in line with the interests of Türkiye.
